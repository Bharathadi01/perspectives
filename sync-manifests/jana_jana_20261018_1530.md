## 1. Summary of Changes
Makes connecting feel like a moment and keeps connections meaningful. A "You're connected" screen with our own chime, a vibration and motion, then straight into the chat with the keyboard up; conversation ideas in an empty chat; quiet connections close on their own with fair, honest notices. Post categories grow from 6 to 11, Explore hides posts you already answered, and a missing migration from the previous push is added.

## 8. Addendum: both must give (ADR-016)
To accept a connect request the receiver must have given the asker a perspective (`PERSPECTIVE_REQUIRED_BEFORE_ACCEPT`, 403). Ticking Connect while giving to someone who already asked accepts their request (`connect.status: 'connected'`). Inbox requests carry `gaveBack`; `Connection.reply` carries the accepter's perspective so the chat shows both. Changed: `domain/connect.ts` (`evaluateAccept`), `connect.service.ts` (`tryConnect`, accept gate, `reply`), `perspectives.service.ts`, contracts (`connect.ts`, `perspective.ts`, `error.ts`), the Inbox, profile and composer screens, the chat card. Tests: 47 unit, 26 end-to-end (the accept tests now give back first). No migration. Docs: `PRODUCT.md`, `API_CONTRACTS.md`, ADR-016.
